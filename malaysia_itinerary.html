📌 Fact-check note — bus fare correction

The original itinerary listed RM 64–72 for the KL ↔ Malacca round trip bus for 2. Actual fares start from RM 10/person one-way — expect RM 40–56 for 2 round trip depending on operator. Book in advance on BusOnlineTicket.com or Easybook.com.

Tips
💳Get a Touch 'n Go card at any train station on arrival — discounts on LRT/MRT and faster boarding.
🗓️Book the Malacca bus in advance — July 14 is a Sunday and buses fill up fast.
⏰KTM Komuter to Batu Caves runs ~every 30 minutes — check the schedule before leaving.
📱Download the KLIA Ekspres app for 10% off KLIA Transit tickets (saves RM 1.20).
🦀At Jalan Alor: avoid seafood platters, crab, and big tourist sets to stay on budget.
☕Coffee rule: 1 shared coffee only — not 2 separate orders — per the budget.
