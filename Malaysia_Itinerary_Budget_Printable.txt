STATION / PLACE CHEAT SHEET
============================================================

[HOTEL] Travelodge Kuala Lumpur City Centre
- Your hotel near Chinatown / Central Market.

[STATION] Pasar Seni
- The train station near your hotel.
- Your "home station" in Kuala Lumpur.
- When you see "Pasar Seni", think: station beside Travelodge / Chinatown area.

[STATION] KL Sentral
- Kuala Lumpur's main transport hub.
- Think of it as the big central train station.
- You transfer here for Batu Caves train and KLIA Transit.

[STATION] Batu Caves
- Train station beside Batu Caves.
- Get off here for Batu Caves.

[STATION] Bandar Tasik Selatan
- Train station beside TBS.
- Get off here when going to the Malacca bus terminal.

[BUS TERMINAL] TBS / Terminal Bersepadu Selatan
- Big intercity bus terminal in Kuala Lumpur.
- This is where you ride the bus to Malacca.

[BUS TERMINAL] Melaka Sentral
- Main bus terminal in Malacca.
- KL to Malacca buses usually stop here.
- From here, take Grab/taxi to Dutch Square / Christ Church.
